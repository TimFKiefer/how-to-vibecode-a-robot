    The build guide

    Easy step-by-step tutorial in 3D: every part in order, with the parts from the CAD model. It is a draft: nobody has built Takt yet, and the guide will be corrected after the first real build.

    Open the build guide →
    01Step 01The build guide

    The robot: Takt

    • 18 smart servos (Feetech STS3215), three per leg. Each reports its angle, speed and load: almost everything the robot will ever feel.
    • Six legs, each with a hip, a thigh and a 120 mm curved blade as the lower leg
    • Belly height from 30 to 95 mm, and Puls is meant to pick it itself
    • Inside: a battery, a small computer, a servo bus board, a camera and a small face display; a balance sensor still has to be fitted
    • 2,051 g in the CAD model, with as many 3D-printed parts as possible

    The brain: Puls

    • A small model that learns this body by itself. Nobody programs a way of walking into it.
    • Starts from zero and has to find its walking rhythm itself
    • Feels only what the real robot can measure
    • Runs on a normal computer, CPU only, all local
    • Counts only if it beats a hand-written gait and a standard learning method (PPO)
